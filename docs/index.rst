LoseMe - Local Semantic Memory
==================================

**Privacy-preserving semantic search over your personal files. Runs entirely on your own hardware.**

LoseMe lets you search documents, emails, and notes by **meaning** rather than filename or keyword.
It builds a semantic index on top of your existing files — originals stay where they are and can be 
opened directly from search results. All processing happens on your machine. Nothing is sent to external services.
